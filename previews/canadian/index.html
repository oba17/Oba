About Me

I’m a dedicated Senior Salesforce Administrator with a passion for transforming complex business challenges into efficient, scalable solutions. With broad experience across multiple industries, I specialize in maximizing platform utility to drive operational excellence, improve user experience, and ensure robust data governance. My approach blends technical proficiency with strategic thinking, always prioritizing stakeholder collaboration and intelligent automation.

I began my career as a lawyer, called to the Nigerian Bar in 2012, advocating in courts up to the Supreme Court. While leading business development at a law firm, I discovered Salesforce—and never looked back. Since then, I’ve built impactful, user-focused systems that deliver lasting value.
