Projects / Employment Services Job Target Standardization & Placement Intelligence

01 / Executive perspective

Solving a data governance problem at the heart of employment service delivery.

Before the transformation, staff captured occupational aspirations in free text. Around 40,000 historical manually entered titles contained inconsistent descriptions and duplicates, undermining data quality, client-goal tracking and funder reporting.

I led requirements discovery, future-state process design, solution architecture and technical delivery to establish a reusable occupational reference model anchored to Canada's National Occupational Classification (NOC). Client-specific job targets, postings, candidate submissions and placements could then be connected through standardized records.

Project at a glance

Role: Senior Salesforce Technical Administrator · Project Lead · Solution Architect

Team: Nine technical, functional and business contributors

Timeline: Three months, initiation through deployment and training

Platform: Salesforce Sales Cloud, Custom objects, Screen Flows, Record-triggered flows, Validation Rules, Permission sets, Formula fields, Reports and dashboards

02 / Business challenge

The organization could record employment aspirations—but could not reliably use them.

The issue was larger than inconsistent terminology. Free-text records limited reliable matching, a structured history of client goals, and defensible reporting to funding partners.

Fragmented occupational data

Similar career aspirations were entered under different descriptions, creating duplicate values and inconsistent classifications.

Manual matching

Staff interpreted client interests and employer opportunities without a dependable shared classification key.

Limited traceability

Primary targets, candidate submissions and placements were difficult to reconcile against original employment goals.

04 / Salesforce solution architecture

Separating occupational master data from client-specific goals unlocked reusable intelligence.

The standardized Job Target object provides the reference vocabulary. Client Job Target records represent each individual's employment goals; shared NOC codes enable posting alignment while preserving links to submissions and employment outcomes.

Making matching faster, more consistent and measurable.

Reduced administration

Guided multi-record capture reduced repeated navigation and manual data entry; the project reports more than a 70% reduction in administrative effort.

Instant occupational visibility

When a Job Posting and an active Client Job Target share the relevant NOC code, related lists surface the match immediately. The project reports more than an 80% improvement in operational matching.

Traceable placement outcomes

Linked Job Target, Posting, Submission and Placement records strengthen evidence that employment aligns with career goals. The project reports more than a 90% improvement in matching placements to desired targets.

Measurement transparency: The percentages above are project-reported outcomes. Before/after benchmark datasets and measurement periods have not been supplied, so they are not reconstructed or treated as independently verified. Record counts are reported operational snapshots, not evidence of causal changes in placement success. NOC matching establishes occupational-code alignment, not by itself whether every candidate is suitable for a role.
