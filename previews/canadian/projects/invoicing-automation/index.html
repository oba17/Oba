Projects / Invoicing Automation System

01 / Executive overview

Making monthly invoicing more accurate, consistent and auditable.

Government- and privately-funded employment programs relied on service delivery records to calculate billable activity. The legacy process involved manual Excel reporting, fragmented reconciliation and inconsistent source information.

The project team implemented a Salesforce solution connecting funded Programs, Program Components, Service Elements, monthly Deliverable Reporting and Invoice records. Approval and automation workflows improved control over billing preparation and financial reporting. Invoices were generated as Salesforce records, exported to a finance application, and then sent to funders by that application in a standard accounting format.

My role was Salesforce Administrator and QA Lead—not the overall project lead. I configured selected fields and page layouts, updated flows, coordinated user acceptance testing and defects, led historical data cleanup and trained end users across the organization.

Engagement at a glance

02 / The business challenge

Billing preparation depended on disconnected reports and manual reconciliation.

The need was to establish reliable billable service information, control monthly review and maintain traceability from program delivery through invoice preparation and payment updates.

Fragmented monthly reporting

Finance depended on manual Excel consolidation rather than structured Salesforce reporting cycles.

Legacy data quality

Historical records needed substantial cleanup to support accurate reporting and reliable automation.

Complex status updates

Marking an invoice paid could affect up to approximately 1,000 associated records, requiring careful processing design and testing.

03 / Future-state process

Connecting delivery records, approvals, invoices and payment status.

The project established a controlled Salesforce billing-preparation workflow. Salesforce-generated invoice records were exported to a finance application, which formatted and sent them to funders.

01 / SCHEDULE

Generate monthly reporting

Screen Flows create reporting schedules for program components.

02 / REVIEW

Approve deliverables

Program and Finance stakeholders review billable monthly activity.

03 / CREATE

Prepare Salesforce invoice

Finance uses a guided Flow to aggregate eligible approved schedules and invoice totals.

04 / EXPORT & SUBMIT

Finance application

Salesforce invoice records are exported to the finance application, which sends them to funders in a standard accounting format.

05 / UPDATE

Synchronize payment status

Salesforce invoice and related service records reflect payment completion through internal update workflows.

Implemented Apex batch processing / high-volume paid-status updates

Marking an invoice as paid could require updating approximately 1,000 related records. I proposed using Apex batch processing to handle these high-volume updates, and the approach was implemented as part of the solution.
