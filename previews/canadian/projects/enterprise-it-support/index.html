Projects / Enterprise IT Support Ticketing and Service Management Transformation

01 / Executive perspective

Changing the operating model—not simply introducing a ticketing tool.

The original support process relied on emails and informal interactions, making ownership, response times and management reporting difficult to control. I led the redesign into a structured Salesforce Case lifecycle with consistent intake, ownership, status communications and reporting.

The delivery combined process discovery, requirements and acceptance criteria, Salesforce-native architecture, technical implementation, quality assurance, deployment readiness and user adoption.

Project at a glance

Role: Senior Salesforce Business Analyst · Solution Architect · Technical Lead

Delivery team: Five cross-functional contributors

Platform: Salesforce Service Cloud

Capabilities: Screen Flows, Email-to-Case, Case queues, assignment rules, record-triggered flows, Apex, operational reports and dashboards

02 / Business problem

The challenge: fragmented requests, manual triage and limited accountability.

The team could resolve individual issues, but support demand was not consistently captured or managed as an end-to-end service operation.

Inconsistent intake

Requests arrived via email and ad hoc conversations, often without a clear subject, classification or priority.

Unclear ownership

Manual assignment slowed triage and made escalations, follow-ups and request status difficult to track.

Incomplete intelligence

Fragmented conversation histories and limited reporting restricted management's view of demand and performance.

03 / Process redesign

A single Case lifecycle—from guided intake to resolution.

I designed a requester-to-agent service journey built around Salesforce Cases, supported by automated notifications and structured communication capture.

To-Be / Service Cloud operating process

01 / INTAKE

Guided request

Utility item opens a Screen Flow; users submit details and files or links.

02 / RECORD

Case created

Requester and support queue receive confirmations.

03 / ROUTE

Queue ownership

Assignment automation and agent self-selection.

04 / COLLABORATE

Tracked replies

Email-to-Case and Apex clean and capture follow-up content.

05 / RESOLVE

Close and measure

Resolution notice and KPI dashboard updates.

Additional-information path: When a support agent populates an information-request field and saves, automation emails the requester. The requester replies to the existing case email thread; Email-to-Case relates the message to the Case and an Apex-supported process captures the relevant body content for follow-up.

04 / Salesforce-native architecture

Designing for clarity, automation and long-term maintainability.

Salesforce Case acts as the service record, with related EmailMessage records, users and queues, and linked documents. Native platform features handle most routing and lifecycle events; Apex provides the targeted email-content processing that the business needed.
