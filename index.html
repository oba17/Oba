About Me

Bridging Business Strategy, Technology & Meaningful Transformation

I am a Senior Salesforce Professional, Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP®), and Solution Architect with over fifteen years of experience translating complex business challenges into practical, scalable, and sustainable technology solutions. My expertise spans business analysis, enterprise process transformation, Salesforce architecture and administration, systems integration, and end-to-end solution delivery.

My professional journey began in law, where I represented clients, interpreted complex legislation, assessed risks, and developed strategies to resolve challenging legal and business matters. Practising law taught me that effective solutions begin with attentive listening, rigorous analysis, sound judgment, and a clear understanding of the issues beneath the surface. These principles continue to define my approach to technology and business transformation.

While leading business development initiatives in legal practice, I discovered the transformative potential of Salesforce. What began as an interest in improving business operations evolved into a career dedicated to connecting organizational needs with technological capabilities. I brought my legal training in critical thinking, stakeholder advocacy, and structured problem-solving into business analysis, solution architecture, and technical delivery.

Today, I work across the full solution lifecycle—from business discovery, requirements engineering, and process modelling to architecture, platform configuration, automation, integrations, deployment, and continuous improvement. I combine the strategic perspective of a business analyst with the technical depth of a Salesforce specialist, ensuring that solutions are not only well-engineered but also commercially relevant, secure, intuitive, and aligned with organizational objectives.

Across commercial, consulting, and nonprofit environments, I have led cross-functional initiatives that streamline operations, strengthen governance, improve service delivery, and enable measurable business growth. I bring intellectual curiosity, meticulous attention to detail, and a strong sense of ownership to every engagement, approaching each challenge with the conviction that the right solution must address the underlying problem, not merely its symptoms.

Why I Do This

Solving the Right Problems. Building What Matters. Creating Lasting Value.

What drives me is the opportunity to turn complexity into clarity and clarity into meaningful progress.

Throughout my career, I have been drawn to problems that demand more than an immediate answer. In law, that meant listening carefully to a client's concerns, examining the facts, interpreting the applicable legal framework, and developing a compelling path toward resolution. In business analysis and technology, the discipline remains remarkably similar: understand the people, uncover the underlying problem, evaluate the possibilities, and design a solution that delivers the intended outcome.

I believe technology should serve a greater purpose than automating tasks or introducing new systems. It should remove barriers, strengthen decision-making, empower people, and give organizations the capacity to achieve more. Whether improving revenue operations, simplifying service delivery, strengthening data governance, or connecting fragmented systems, I find fulfilment in building solutions that make work more effective and create tangible value for the people who depend on them.

My approach is grounded in a commitment to understand before designing, challenge assumptions before deciding, and measure outcomes beyond implementation. I am naturally inquisitive, persistent in investigating root causes, and deliberate in balancing business priorities with technical feasibility, risk, scalability, and long-term sustainability.

Equally important is my commitment to continuous growth. I see every complex challenge as an opportunity to deepen my expertise, broaden my perspective, and become a better strategist, architect, and leader. I invest in learning not simply to master new technologies, but to develop the judgment and adaptability required to solve increasingly consequential business problems.

Ultimately, my motivation is straightforward: to build solutions that work, empower people to succeed, and leave organizations stronger than I found them.
